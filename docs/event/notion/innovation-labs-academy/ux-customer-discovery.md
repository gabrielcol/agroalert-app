# UX & Customer Discovery

### **Why This Matters - Why Startups Fail**

Look up "why startups fail" anywhere and you'll get the same top-20 list. The #1 reason, across every industry:

**Inability to understand market and customer demand.** You build something for nothing, for no one - and no one buys it.

Why it happens: we build based on **what we** _**think**_ **is needed**. What we think and what's real can be two very different things. If you don't validate before you build, you can spend time and tokens building something nobody pays for (or even use).

Other common killers:

- **Running out of cash.** €100k or €1M becomes a _runway_, not a fortune. Even with clients in the pipeline, you close if you can't pay the servers.
- **Failing to build and hold a team.** Leadership is hard everywhere: startups, NGOs, all of it.-
  This module makes sure you're **building the right thing**.
