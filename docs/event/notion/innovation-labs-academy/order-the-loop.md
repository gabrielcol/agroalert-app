# Order the Loop

Submit your response: [https://forms.gle/pCWRx3jbggqKoyK28](https://forms.gle/pCWRx3jbggqKoyK28)

**Base version**

**Instructions:** Put these five steps in the order the discovery loop actually runs.

- Uncover insights
- Define a hypothesis
- Discover a market need
- Act on the insights
- Do the research
  **Advanced layer**

**Instructions:** Each scenario describes a founder stuck at one point in the loop, without realizing it. Say which step they're skipping or stuck on, and what they should do instead.

1. "I built a prototype for a month, and I keep working on it while I look for people to test it with."
1. "I ran 10 interviews, wrote down what people said, and I'm already coding the full feature based on the first three quotes that agreed with me."
1. "We keep changing our target customer every week based on the last person we spoke to."
