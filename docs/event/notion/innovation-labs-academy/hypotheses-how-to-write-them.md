# Hypotheses: how to write them

A hypothesis says: _our proposed solution solves [problem] with [solution]._ Good hypotheses are:

- **Testable** - you can walk into an interview and come out with a clear validated / invalidated
- **Clear and specific**

##### They can be about any of these areas:

- **Product** - what to put in it
- **Customer** - who has the need
- **Channel** - web, mobile, prompt, etc.
- **Pricing** - how much, whether they'd pay at all
- **Market** - which market you're moving toward
- **Competition** - why competitors built what they did
- **Feasibility** - _can_ we build it; _should_ we

##### Fast example: **Pricing hypothesis**

- **Assumption:** Students will pay for the product.
- **Problem:** Students often have limited budgets, and the money they spend may not be their own.
- **What to test:** Are they actually willing and able to pay?
  “Students will pay” is a **hypothesis, not a fact**.

**Don't go in trying to** _**confirm**_ **your hypothesis - that's lying to yourself, and it shows later.**
