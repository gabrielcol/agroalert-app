# Innovation Labs Academy x Civic Producthon

### **Welcome, builders!💡**

This year, Innovation Labs is the knowledge provider. You've got one week to prep your challenge, understand the problem you're solving, and put the learning mechanics to work, so you walk into hackathon day with full context.

### What you'll do here

1. **Go through the theory:** UX, Customer Discovery, Experiments, Personas
1. **Run the learning mechanics:** hands-on exercises that force you to apply what you just read. This is where you catch what a good hypothesis or a non-leading question looks like, before you write your own.
1. **Build your assignments:** only two this round to prepare with your team: your **Hypotheses** and your **Interview Guide**. Those are what get reviewed before Producthon.
1. **Optional**, but **very important**: Run interviews, find people on Linkedin, ask around for recommendations. Afterwards create the **user persona** profile. This will be your unfair advantage for the hackathon.
