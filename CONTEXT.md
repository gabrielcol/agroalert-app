# AgroPlan — domain glossary

Canonical English terms used in code, with the Romanian term farmers see in the UI.
Glossary only: no implementation details.

| Term                       | Romanian                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Crop**                   | Cultură                 | A species grown on the field: winter wheat, barley, rapeseed, maize.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Variety**                | Soi                     | A named cultivar of a Crop (Glosa, Pitar, Ursita for wheat; P0216 for maize).                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Field Profile**          | Teren                   | What the farmer tells us about their land: village/commune (resolved to a Field Location), land-size bucket (under 5 ha, 5 to 10 ha, over 10 ha), whether they can irrigate, and the Soil Class.                                                                                         |
| **Field Location**         | Locația terenului       | The point (latitude, longitude) that stands for the farmer's parcel, resolved from the village name they typed or from their phone's position. All weather data is looked up for this point.                                                                                             |
| **Soil Class**             | Tipul de sol            | The farmer's own description of their soil, one of: cernoziom, lutos, argilos, nisipos, or unknown. A tap, not a lab result.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Climate Profile**        | Profilul climatic       | What the last ten full calendar years of weather looked like at a Field Location, summarised: monthly normals (temperature, rainfall, evapotranspiration, water balance), frost and heat statistics, growing-degree-day accumulation, and a per-year table that flags drought years.     |
| **Current Season**         | Sezonul curent          | The weather of the current calendar year to date at the Field Location, shown against the Climate Profile so anomalies stand out, plus the last thirty days day by day.                                                                                                                  |
| **Forecast**               | Prognoza                | The day-by-day weather expected at the Field Location for the next sixteen days. The first seven days are the trusted part.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Seasonal Outlook**       | Tendința sezonieră      | Week-by-week tendencies (warmer/colder, wetter/drier than the Climate Profile) for weeks three to seven ahead. An area-level tendency with low confidence, never a local forecast.                                                                                                       |
| **Weather Brief**          | Sinteza meteo           | The bundle handed to the AI for one Field Profile: Climate Profile, Current Season, Forecast and Seasonal Outlook, with today's date.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Crop Dictionary**        | Dicționarul culturilor  | The fixed reference list of field crops that can be grown in Romania, with each crop's agronomic requirements, calendar, rotation and Varieties. The AI recommends only from this list.                                                                                                  |
| **Crop Recommendation**    | Recomandarea de culturi | The AI's ranked answer for a Field Profile: the top three Crops with a fit score, reasons, risks, a suggested Sowing Window and confidence, plus the Crops it set aside and why. Only Crops whose Sowing Window is open now or opens within the Seasonal Outlook horizon are candidates. |
| **Variety Recommendation** | Recomandarea de soiuri  | For the Crop the farmer chose: every Variety of that Crop in the Crop Dictionary ranked with fit and reasons.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Sowing Plan**            | Plan de semănat         | Exactly one Crop + one Variety for a Field Profile, with a Sowing Window, a Crop Calendar, Alerts and, once confirmed, a Sowing Date.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sowing Window**          | Fereastra de semănat    | The date range in which sowing is recommended for the Plan's area, derived from the forecast.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Sowing Date**            | Data semănatului        | The day the farmer confirms the seeds went in the ground for a Sowing Plan. Recorded once; it is the anchor from which every Stage date is computed.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Crop Calendar**          | Calendarul culturii     | The ordered Stages of a Sowing Plan, starting at the Sowing Date. Each Stage after the first is placed a number of days after the previous Stage.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Stage**                  | Etapă                   | One operation in a Crop Calendar (sowing, emergence, spring work, treatments, harvest). A Stage has a title and a day offset from the previous Stage; its calendar date exists only once the Sowing Date is known.                                                                       |
| **Alert**                  | Alertă                  | A notification tied to a Sowing Plan: drought in the Sowing Window, suitable rain, ANM warning code status.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **ANM**                    | ANM                     | Administrația Națională de Meteorologie, the national weather service whose warning codes Alerts relay.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Eval Case**              | Caz de evaluare         | A Field Profile, a Weather Brief and a date, together with the constraints a correct Crop Recommendation or Variety Recommendation for them must satisfy.                                                                                                                                |
| **Captured Brief**         | Brief captat            | A Weather Brief frozen from the live weather source at a known date, used unchanged as eval input.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Synthetic Brief**        | Brief sintetic          | A Weather Brief generated rather than observed, labelled as such, used where no real brief for that date can exist.                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Notes

- The dashboard ("Culturile tale") lists **Sowing Plans**, not Crops: the same Crop can
  appear twice with different Varieties.
- "Recommended" marks the Crop or Variety ranked first in a Crop Recommendation or Variety
  Recommendation; it is a suggestion, the farmer still chooses.
- The Sowing Window in a Crop Recommendation is derived from the Crop Dictionary's windows
  for the area, checked against the Forecast and Seasonal Outlook.
- A Sowing Plan exists only from the moment the farmer confirms the Sowing Date; before
  that the wizard shows a proposal, not a Plan.
- Alerts are on for every Sowing Plan; there is no separate subscription step.
